GridCraft Local 0.9.0 — independent Windows x64 local edition 1

Portable: extract the entire ZIP to a writable local folder, then run GridCraftLocal.exe.
Keep the adjacent GridCraftLocal.portable marker: settings are saved in Data beside the EXE.
Installer: run GridCraftLocal-Setup.exe. Installs for your current user, with a Start menu shortcut.
Installed program: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\GridCraftLocal\GridCraftLocal.exe
Installed settings/logs: %LOCALAPPDATA%\GridCraftLocal
Uninstall through Windows Installed Apps. Your documents and profile are preserved.

XLSX editing, formulas, charts, CSV/TSV and ODS import, PDF/HTML export. Legacy XLS/XLSB inputs and VBA projects are disabled in this edition.
Automation/control listeners and external-link launching are disabled. No updater or account connection is installed. Windows printing remains an explicit user action.
The app accepts regular files up to 128 MiB; package members are capped at 64 MiB and the expanded package tree at 256 MiB. Macros, ActiveX, opaque embedded OLE content, and external workbook data-refresh connections are refused. Embedded chart workbooks are capped at 8 MiB and inspected recursively. Direct UNC/device/URL paths and alternate data streams are refused.

These are unsigned community builds. No signing certificate or system-wide trust exception has been installed. Scanning and tests reduce risk but cannot establish that every possible file or behavior is safe. The application runs with your ordinary user permissions, without an OS-level network sandbox. Work on copies of important documents and keep backups; Office fidelity is incomplete. Files exported here and opened in other software inherit that software's security policy.

Provenance, validation scope, remaining maintenance notices and checksums are in REPORT.html and validation/build-manifest.json in the distribution root. Source is provided separately; upstream copyright and licensing remain in LICENSE-MIT, LICENSE-APACHE and THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt.
